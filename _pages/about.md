I am a senior at BYU studying Mathematics and Computer Science.

I’m interested in both theoretical and applied post-quantum cryptography. Currently I am formalizing and evaluating cryptographic protocols for Utah's new State-Endorsed Digital Identity [(SEDI)](https://sedi.utah.gov/) framework, under the supervision of [Kent Seamons](https://cs.byu.edu/department/directories/faculty-directory/kent-seamons/).

Previously, I was a research intern at Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy (MPI-SP) in cryptographic engineering, supervised by [Peter Schwabe](https://cryptojedi.org/peter/index.shtml). I worked on evaluating ML-KEM at (much) higher security levels, available [here](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2730).

My mathematical interests lie in algebraic number theory and computational algebra, with applications to cryptography. My most recent project involved constructing monogenic fields of cryptographic size, available [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22374). An earlier work on algebraic geometry is available [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.22897).
